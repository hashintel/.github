## HASH

**[HASH]** is a self-building knowledge graph and platform for high-trust data and AI.

### Get started

- Sign up for the free hosted version of HASH at [hash.ai]
- Browse our developer docs and resources at [hash.dev]

### Underlying technology

HASH is made possible by combining a series of technologies:

- The [SemType] standard for universal types that can be understood, used and composed by both people and machines
- The [hgres] high-performance graph engine written in Rust, built around SemType
- The [Petrinaut] framework for Petri Net-based modeling of processes, environments, and distributed systems in general
- Our [Salt] design system and its associated tooling
